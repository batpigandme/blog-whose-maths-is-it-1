# js10

Backs the post's footnote `[^js10]`: "JavaScript 1.0 shipped in Netscape Navigator 2.0 in March 1996, with 17 functions on `Math`. One of them, `Math.random`, was 'available only on Unix platforms,' and the number-parsing functions behaved differently 'depending upon host operating system,' in Wirfs-Brock and Eich's history of the language."

A recorded reading of a published figure, not a run. Nothing here was regenerated.

## Claim

JavaScript 1.0's built-in library listed 17 `Math` functions and 8 `Math` constants. The figure marks `Math.random` with a footnote that says it was available only on Unix platforms in 1.0, and marks the number-parsing functions `parseFloat` and `parseInt` with a footnote that says their behavior depended on the host operating system.
